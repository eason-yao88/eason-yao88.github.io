Project interests

I'm interested in how public discourse around AI has shaped and will continue to shape U.S. elections, dividing politicians in unexpected ways as they try to adjust to voter sentiment around everything from data centers to AI agents.

I'm also interested in how the use of AI has impacted political campaigns and how it shapes messaging.

I'm also open to more technical projects that look at frontier developments in physical AI and how that affects the politics of AI.
